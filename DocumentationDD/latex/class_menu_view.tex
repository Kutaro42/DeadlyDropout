\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
View responsible for rendering and handling clicks on the main screen. 

Kept intentionally thin:\+ it owns no business logic and emits a signal when the player clicks "{}\+Play"{}, delegating the decision of what to do next entirely to \doxylink{class_menu_controller}{Menu\+Controller}. This separation means the visual layout can be redesigned without touching any game-\/state code.

Renders the background image, the game logo and the Play/\+\+Quit buttons.
