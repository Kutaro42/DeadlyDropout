\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Model encapsulating the player\textquotesingle{}s properties, resources and actions. 

Manages HP, speed, gold, keys and cardinal orientation. \doxylink{class_katana}{Katana} actions are delegated to the internal instance.

The cardinal orientation (facing\+Dir) is determined either by keyboard movement or by the attack angle passed to \doxylink{class_player_model_a3e403a4d837cd7b6c0eb49370dfc3eea}{launch\+Katana()}. Priority rule:\+ if the player is attacking, the cardinal direction is the one closest to the attack angle; otherwise it follows the keyboard.
