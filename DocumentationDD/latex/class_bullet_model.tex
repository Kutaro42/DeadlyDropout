\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\doxylink{class_projectile}{Projectile} fired by the player (semi-\/automatic pistol). 

Inherits from \doxylink{class_projectile}{Projectile}:\+ moves in a straight line at a constant speed. Deactivates (set\+Active(false)) upon collision with a wall or an enemy, handled by \doxylink{class_level_controller_a1390cb51714507a3e13885c7b1d95605}{Level\+Controller::\+check\+Bullet\+Collisions()}.

Damage:\+ 12 base points + adaptive bonus 2–5 + critical hit ×2 (GDD). Speed:\+ 500 px/\+s (GDD).
