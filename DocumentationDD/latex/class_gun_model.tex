\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages firing logic, magazine, ammo reserve and shoot cooldown (GDD Step 5). 

GDD ammunition rules:\+
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Semi-\/auto:\+ 1 click = 1 shot, 0.\+5 s cooldown between shots.
\item Magazine (mag) + general reserve (reserve).
\item Press R to reload:\+ fills the magazine from reserve, preserving existing rounds.
\item Damage:\+ 12 base + random 2–5 bonus + 2 \% critical chance × 2 multiplier.
\item Bullet speed:\+ 500 px/\+s.
\end{DoxyItemize}

\doxylink{class_bullet_model}{Bullet\+Model} instances are owned by \doxylink{class_gun_model}{Gun\+Model} and updated every tick. \doxylink{class_level_controller}{Level\+Controller} accesses \doxylink{class_gun_model_a0163b15d49a3fdb6cbef3654a346e732}{get\+Bullets()} to resolve collision with enemies.

Sound effects are allocated here rather than in the view because the trigger condition (successful shot /\+ empty mag) is a model-\/level event, and playing audio in the view would require it to poll the model state every frame.
