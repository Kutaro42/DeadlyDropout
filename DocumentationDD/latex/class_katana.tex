\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Model of the player\textquotesingle{}s melee weapon (GDD Step 5 — Combat rules). 

Manages weapon statistics (damage, range), its collision zone (Slash\+Zone), and timers for cooldown and animation.

Design decision — free-\/angle slash:\+ The Slash\+Zone is computed from an arbitrary angle (radians) passed to \doxylink{class_katana_aa1b0b7ffb114657c094e97c97df9cc58}{try\+Attack()}. This allows attacking in any direction (not just the 4 cardinals), matching the GDD requirement that the katana targets "{}enemies within the player\textquotesingle{}s range zone"{} oriented toward the mouse cursor.

The AABB returned by \doxylink{class_katana_aca178b00a3a69321c2b209d8fde7f4da}{get\+Slash\+Zone()} is the bounding box of the oriented slash rectangle, ensuring compatibility with the existing AABB collision tests in \doxylink{class_level_controller}{Level\+Controller} without requiring a full OBB intersection algorithm.

The attack angle is exposed via \doxylink{class_katana_a23f153c60311b77b53f7f6256129d0ed}{get\+Attack\+Angle()} so \doxylink{class_player_view}{Player\+View} can orient the slash sprite effect correctly.

Computes the AABB enclosing the oriented slash rectangle for a given angle. 

The logical slash rectangle has dimensions KATANA\+\_\+\+SLASH\+\_\+W × its\+Range. Its near edge starts at the player\textquotesingle{}s hitbox boundary (not at the centre) to avoid hitting enemies directly behind the player. The four corners are rotated by angle\+Rad and their axis-\/aligned bounding box is computed to produce the final AABB.
